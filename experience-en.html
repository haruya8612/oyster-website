Staff's recommended menu

Assorted raw oysters

We offer three types of seasonal oysters carefully selected from all over the country. This is our most popular menu item, where you can taste and compare the different production areas and flavors of each product. You can enjoy it with your favorite seasonings such as lemon, ponzu sauce, and special sauce.

Oysters steamed in wine

Wine-steamed oysters with a crisp texture and delicious oyster flavor. The aroma of wine and thyme brings out the deliciousness of the oysters.

Oyster and sea urchin cream risotto

A creamy risotto that combines the deliciousness of oysters and the rich flavor of sea urchin. The harmonious combination of al dente rice and the bounty of the sea is an unforgettable taste. This item is also very popular.

oyster set 12P, 16P

An assortment of carefully selected raw and grilled oysters from all over the country. We offer 12-piece or 16-piece sets that allow you to enjoy the characteristics of the production area and taste. Enjoy the original deliciousness of oysters with our special sauce and lemon.
